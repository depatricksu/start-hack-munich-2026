Line for the pitch
Today a forwarder gets the yes and then types the same shipment into seven more mails. Our agent already has every field, so it writes them: the truck is ordered, the airline has the details, the losing bidders are thanked, and the customer hears about every step in her own language.

Same rule as tonight: code fills in the facts, the AI writes the words, a person approves anything that changes money or dates.

Why this fits 5U's brief: "one mailbox that quotes, books and tracks on its own". These are the mails the same mailbox would send next. No new system, no new data.
