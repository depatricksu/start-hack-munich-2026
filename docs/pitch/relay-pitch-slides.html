The problem · 02 / 07

Freight quoting stops overnight.

A forwarder buys air transport for a customer: read the request, find the best price from airlines and truckers, quote, book, track. After hours the mail just waits — and by morning the customer has asked three others.
02:00 — the request

A textile company in Hamburg needs a price to Delhi, with a truck from their own site. Cheapest option, please.

Relay's reply
62 seconds later

One all-in price, in the customer's language, in their own thread. Says yes → booked. Asks where it is → answered.

Reads anything · 04 / 07

The request can arrive as anything.

Every format, one pipeline

  • PDF & Word requests and packing lists, parsed into the same fields as a plain mail.
  • Voice messages transcribed on-device, then quoted like any other request.
  • Photos read with on-device text recognition — UN numbers, classes, lithium & aerosols — even when the mail says "non-dangerous".
PDFWordExcel Voice .mp3Photo OCR

The control room

  • Every conversation, live: route, facts, price options, the full thread with its attachments.
  • Unclear or unusual cargo surfaces as "Needs you", with the whole history in one place.
  • Prompt-injection safe: prices, links and payment details are always replaced by our own template — the price stays ours.

Hardened live · 05 / 07

5U changed the rules every hour. We treated every lost round as data.

Dangerous goods, hidden · fixed fast
17:00 — cargo was dangerous in the attachment while the mail said "non-dangerous". Relay now reads every attachment and asks for the safety papers.
Rate portal went down · minutes
18:40 — the portal returned 503. Relay fell back to live carrier bids by email within minutes and kept quoting.
"Do not offer this airline" · round won
A customer excluded an airline mid-thread. No team passed that round. Relay now never even asks the excluded carrier.

How we built it · 06 / 07

Four people, four AI agents, one repo, one night.

One folder per owner, agents coordinating in a shared issue with CLAIM / DONE / NEED. Only one machine may send mail; every other runs dry. A fix goes live only after all tests and a fake quote pass — so a bad change can't take the desk down.
